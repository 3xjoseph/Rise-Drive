# Rise-Drive
Links: https://sharemygame.com/@3xjoseph/rise-drive

Rise Dive is an exhilarating 3D platformer that takes you on a thrilling journey through the cosmos. As the captain of a stranded rocketship, your mission is to navigate through a series of challenging obstacles and treacherous terrains to find your way back to your home planet. Explore mysterious alien worlds, dodge asteroids, and overcome gravity-defying challenges as you rise to new heights and dive through the unknown.
